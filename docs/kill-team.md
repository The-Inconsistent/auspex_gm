# Deathwatch Kill Team — Tier 4 Primaris, built from the books

The user picked five operatives from GW's *Kill Team* Deathwatch rules (Watch Sergeant, Disruptor, Horde-Slayer,
Marksman, Blademaster). Kill Team is a different game, so each operative is mapped onto a Wrath & Glory
archetype and wargear. Pages: **VoA** = Vow of Absolution (printed = PDF page); **Core** = core rulebook (PDF = printed + 1).

## What the books say about the Deathwatch

**No Deathwatch rules exist** in the core rulebook or Vow of Absolution: no keyword, archetype or ammo rule.
The only mentions:
- Core p.19 cites a Deathwatch Kill Team as its example of a high-Tier group.
- Core p.64 says the Ordo Xenos often work alongside the Deathwatch.
- Core p.303: the Deathwatch keep a station on Enoch's northern pole, in the Gilead System, and operate in kill
  teams.

Everything Deathwatch-specific below is a **GM choice**, marked [GM].

## Tier 4 Primaris options as written (Core p.20–22, VoA pp.26–43)

Tier 4 characters start with 400 XP.

**Tier 4 archetypes with the PRIMARIS keyword:**
- Intercessor (Core p.117, 300 XP; VoA p.34 is misprinted)
- Eliminator (VoA p.32, 276 XP)
- Incursor (VoA p.33, 273 XP)
- Reiver (VoA p.37, 288 XP)

**Other routes:**
- A fifth Primaris build: Tactical Space Marine (Tier 3, 277 XP) + **Rubicon Primaris** (VoA p.43, 80 XP). This
  gives the PRIMARIS keyword, Bolter Discipline, Built Tough, +2 Influence and 1 Uncommon Wargear.
- Bladeguard Veteran and Primaris Lieutenant are **Tier 5** (VoA pp.40–41). A Primaris Apothecary, Chaplain or
  Librarian is also Tier 5 (VoA p.23).
- Doctrines of Death (Heavy Intercessor, Hellblaster, etc., VoA p.35, 60 XP) are bought **during play**, and only by
  an Intercessor.

## GM decisions (2026-09-24)

1. **Species**: all five are **Primaris Astartes** (+3 Wounds, 22 implants, ignore gene-seed flaws, Core p.29), including
   the Eliminator, Incursor and Reiver, whose pages list "Adeptus Astartes".
2. **Deathwatch armoury**: all the loadout swaps in the table above are allowed at creation.
3. **Chapter**: each marine keeps their parent Chapter's [CHAPTER] keyword and Traits (Core pp.71–74; Primaris ignore the
   "(Gene-seed)" traits), plus a **DEATHWATCH** keyword for Influence with the Ordo Xenos.
4. **Absolvers material**: vows and Sanguine Visions only apply to an Absolvers marine, and are narrative only
   (VoA p.124).

| Foe | Source | Class at Tier 4 |

| Necrons (the tomb) | Threat Assessment pp.131–156 | see each stat block |
| Adeptus Mechanicus | no bestiary entries: Quick NPC table (Core p.326) or custom blocks from Skitarius (p.101) / Tech-Priest (p.109) | GM sets |

Encounter guideline (Threat Assessment p.8): two Troops per Agent, half in a Mob, plus one Elite or Adversary.
For five Space Marines, "optimised groups may require larger, tougher, or more numerous enemies". VoA p.121 adds
that for Space Marines, powerful antagonists should be the highlight, with massed troops "a barrier to their objectives".
