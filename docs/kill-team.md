# Deathwatch Kill Team — Tier 4 Primaris, built from the books

The roster is all eleven operatives from GW's *Kill Team* Deathwatch rules. The players pick five; the rest stay in
reserve at the station. Kill Team is a different game, so each operative is mapped onto a Wrath & Glory archetype and
wargear, and each Kommando operative onto a bestiary stat block, without new rules. Pages: **VoA** = Vow of
Absolution, **TA** = Threat Assessment, **GK** = Gutshiva's Kommandos, **FSPG** = Forsaken System Player's Guide,
**LotL** = Litanies of the Lost (all printed = PDF page); **Core** = core rulebook (PDF = printed + 1); **Apoc** = An
Abundance of Apocrypha, homebrew (PDF = printed + 3).

## What the books say about the Deathwatch

**No Deathwatch rules exist** in the core rulebook or Vow of Absolution: no keyword, archetype or ammo rule.
The only mentions:
- Core p.19 cites a Deathwatch Kill Team as its example of a high-Tier group.
- Core p.64 says the Ordo Xenos often work alongside the Deathwatch.
- Core p.303: the Deathwatch keep a station on Enoch's northern pole, in the Gilead System, and operate in kill
  teams.

The homebrew Apocrypha has a Deathwatch package (Apoc p.211), used here as the Tier 4→5 Ascension (Rulings
2026-09-27). Everything else Deathwatch-specific below is a **GM choice**, marked [GM].

## Tier 4 Primaris options as written (Core p.20–22, VoA pp.26–43, Apoc)

Tier 4 characters start with 400 XP.

**Tier 4 archetypes with the PRIMARIS keyword:**
- Intercessor (Core p.117, 300 XP; VoA p.34 is misprinted)
- Eliminator (VoA p.32, 276 XP)
- Incursor (VoA p.33, 273 XP)
- Reiver (VoA p.37, 288 XP)
- Homebrew: Primaris Aggressor (Apoc p.181), Primaris Hellblaster (Apoc p.183)

**Other routes:**
- Tactical Space Marine (Tier 3, 277 XP) + **Rubicon Primaris** (VoA p.43, 80 XP). This gives the PRIMARIS keyword,
  Bolter Discipline, Built Tough, +2 Influence and 1 Uncommon Wargear.
- Homebrew: Assault Space Marine (Apoc p.137, Tier 3) + Rubicon Primaris.
- Bladeguard Veteran and Primaris Lieutenant are **Tier 5** (VoA pp.40–41). A Primaris Apothecary, Chaplain or
  Librarian is also Tier 5 (VoA p.23).
- Doctrines of Death (Heavy Intercessor, Hellblaster, etc., VoA p.35, 60 XP) are bought **during play**, and only by
  an Intercessor.

## GM decisions (2026-09-24)

1. **Species**: every marine is a **Primaris Astartes** (+3 Wounds, 22 implants, ignore gene-seed flaws, Core p.29),
   including those whose archetype pages list "Adeptus Astartes".
2. **Deathwatch armoury**: all the loadout swaps in the table above are allowed at creation.
3. **Chapter**: each marine keeps their parent Chapter's [CHAPTER] keyword and Traits (Core pp.71–74; Primaris ignore the
   "(Gene-seed)" traits), plus a **DEATHWATCH** keyword for Influence with the Ordo Xenos.
4. **Absolvers material**: vows and Sanguine Visions only apply to an Absolvers marine, and are narrative only
   (VoA p.124).

## Rulings 2026-09-27

1. **Apocrypha homebrew** is allowed for the pregens.
2. **Primaris Intercessor** costs Core p.117's 300 XP, overriding the Vow of Absolution misprint (`docs/rules-flow.md`
   §11.34).
3. **Blademaster** is rebuilt as Tactical Space Marine + Rubicon Primaris; the **Headtaker** takes the Reiver.
4. **Roster**: all 11 Kill Team operatives. The players pick 5, the rest stay in reserve at the station. No one-Gravis
   limit.
5. **Kill Team stand-ins**: infernus heavy bolter = Heavy Bolter (Core p.217); xenophase blade = Power Sword
   (VoA p.62); breacha ram = Power Klaw (Core p.213); throwing knives = Knife (Core p.211, Thrown, not Silent);
   Snipa = Big Shoota (Core p.220) + Magnocular Scope (Core p.228) + Silencer (Core p.229); the Comms Boy keeps the
   Slugga.
6. **Skitarii** use the Tier 4 row of the Quick NPC table (Core p.326).
7. **The Volkus cache** belongs to Cryptek Anephteh (TA pp.133–134). Ka'lalek's raid is stage 5 of a "Waking" track,
   reskinned from LotL p.73's five-stage response track.
8. **Varonius** had no part in it: it was a tellyporta accident.
9. **The Inquisitor** is Marie Von Xardt (FSPG p.26), replacing Maren Solace.
10. **DEATHWATCH keyword** stays free at creation; the Apocrypha Deathwatch package (Apoc p.211) is the Tier 4→5
    Ascension.
11. **Kommandos**: the operative mapping, the Mob specials house rule and the three swap stat blocks below.
12. **Horde-Slayer and Breacher swap people**: the Horde-Slayer is Brother Hesh Tu'var (Salamanders), the Breacher
    Brother Tancred Varn (Imperial Fists). Each keeps its operative, archetype and kit; the name, Chapter, Background,
    personality, objectives and Chapter Talents moved (`docs/pregens.md` §§3, 8).
13. **New Chapters** for nine pregens; each keeps its operative, archetype route and kit (`docs/pregens.md`):
    - Watch Sergeant Qaan Toghril: White Scars (was Space Wolves).
    - Brother Octavius Serren (Disruptor): Ultramarines (was Raven Guard).
    - Brother Aurelian Tarsus (Marksman): **Red Scorpions**, of unknown origin. [GM] The Chapter has **no Chapter Traits**:
      Core p.71 says a Chapter of unknown succession "may take the Chapter Traits of any of the First Founding Chapters",
      and this one takes none. The catalogue has no Red Scorpions record (it is book data), so his build writes RED
      SCORPIONS into [CHAPTER], which the builder flags (rules-flow.md §11.25); the tests pin that one problem.
    - Brother Sariel Voss (Blademaster): Dark Angels (was Blood Angels).
    - Brother Hjalmar Grimsson (Aegis): Space Wolves (was Black Templars, so no longer homebrew).
    - Brother Idrian Kostas (Bombard): Flesh Tearers (Apoc p.28, a Blood Angels Successor; was Crimson Fists).
    - Brother Sigurd Hrafnsson (Demolisher): Space Wolves, Grimsson's Chapter (was Absolvers, so §11.23 and decision 4 no
      longer reach any pregen).
    - Brother Kael Tirrow (Gunner): Emperor's Spears, an Ultramarines Successor (was Iron Hands). The catalogue has no
      Emperor's Spears record, so [GM] his [CHAPTER] is filled with ULTRAMARINES and he takes its traits (Core p.71,
      "Successor Chapters").
    - Brother Lucan Sarrius (Headtaker): Blood Angels (was White Scars).
    The **Ultramarines are now in**, replacing the earlier choice to leave them out: the user chose them for the Disruptor
    and (by succession) the Gunner, and *Pride of Ultramar (Tradition)* applies as printed: "You start each session with 1
    Wrath Point instead of 2" (Core p.74). The app's session start sets every character to Wrath 2 (Core p.164), so the GM
    takes 1 Wrath off those two sheets. Chapter-restricted Talents were swapped: the Disruptor's Wraith-Slipping (Raven
    Guard, VoA p.50, 10 XP) for Codex Innovator (Ultramarines, VoA p.45, 20 XP), paid for by leaving his Ballistic Skill at
    the Incursor's 4; the Headtaker's Raiding Master (White Scars, VoA p.49) for Echo of Sanguinius (Blood Angels, VoA p.46),
    at the same 10 XP. The Blood Angels' and Flesh Tearers' Red Thirst stays
    narrative for Sarrius and Kostas (Primaris ignore Gene-seed traits, Core p.71), except after an Echo of Sanguinius
    vision.
14. **Bombard and Breacher rebuilt** on Primaris Intercessor (Core p.117, 300 XP) + Heavy Intercessor Doctrine (VoA p.35,
    60 XP), like the Horde-Slayer, instead of the Apocrypha Primaris Aggressor. The Doctrine's Heavy Bolter is swapped for
    each one's Deathwatch kit; the Breacher's launcher fires the Intercessor's own grenades. The Bombard stays homebrew
    (Flesh Tearers, Frag Cannon); the Breacher needs none.

## Kommandos

The Kommando operatives map onto bestiary blocks. `combatant.addThreat` has no per-combatant weapon swap, so each
swap is its own threat file: the Core p.355 Kommando copied exactly, with one change and a `note`.

| Operative | Threat file | Stat block |
|---|---|---|
| Boss Nob | `gutshiva` | Boss Gutshiva (GK p.8) |
| Bomb Squig | `dudd-the-bomb-squig` | Dudd the Bomb Squig (GK p.18) |
| Grot, labelled "Runt" | `grot` | Grot (Core p.358) |
| Boy | `kommando` | Kommando (Core p.355) |
| Burna Boy, Rokkit Boy, Dakka Boy | `kommando` | Kommando with its MORE DAKKA! Mob option: Burna, Rokkit Launcher, Big Shoota (Core p.355) |
| Comms Boy | `kommando` | Kommando, keeps the Slugga; no shokka, no order relay |
| Breacha Boy | `kommando-breacha-boy` | Choppa → Power Klaw (Core p.213, at S 4: 10 +5 ED / AP −3 / Brutal, Unwieldy (3), Waaagh!). Smashing a wall is Shoot Through Cover (Core p.189) |
| Slasha Boy | `kommando-slasha-boy` | adds a Knife as throwing knives (Core p.211: 6 +2 ED, Thrown S×4 = 16 m, not Silent). Twin choppas = the Choppa with Multi-Attack (Core p.187) |
| Snipa Boy | `kommando-snipa-boy` | Slugga → Big Shoota (Core p.220) with Magnocular Scope (Core p.228: Range penalties −2) and Silencer (Core p.229: Silent) |

**House rule: Mob specials.** In this campaign one Kommando in a Mob may take a MORE DAKKA! special weapon for every
**two** Kommandos in the Mob. The book says one per ten (Core p.355).

The swap files keep the MORE DAKKA! text as printed ("while keeping their Choppa and Slugga"), even where the swap
removed one of them. TA p.89 reprints the Kommando with a Threat row, Conviction, Resolve, Speed and Size that look
copied from another block (`ta-kommando.json` note); the campaign fields the Core p.355 block.

## Opposition at Tier 4 (5 players: Glory max 7, Ruin starts at 5, max 10)

| Foe | Source | Class at Tier 4 |
|---|---|---|
| Ork Kommandos | Kommando Core p.355; Boss Gutshiva GK p.8; Dudd GK p.18; Grot Core p.358 | Kommando, Grot and Dudd = **Troop**, so they come in Mobs; Gutshiva = **Elite** |
| Necrons (Cryptek Anephteh's cache) | TA pp.131–156 (Anephteh pp.133–134) | see each stat block |
| Adeptus Mechanicus (Skitarii) | no bestiary entries: the Tier 4 row of the Quick NPC table (Core p.326) | GM sets |

Encounter guideline (TA p.8): two Troops per Agent, half in a Mob, plus one Elite or Adversary. For five Space Marines
that is **Gutshiva + 10 Troops**. TA p.8 adds that "optimised groups may require larger, tougher, or more numerous
enemies", and VoA p.121 that for Space Marines, powerful antagonists should be the highlight, with massed troops "a
barrier to their objectives".
