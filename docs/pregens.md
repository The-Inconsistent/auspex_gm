**Core** = core rulebook, printed page (PDF page = printed + 1). **VoA** = Vow of Absolution (printed page = PDF page;

Why these Chapters: each Chapter trait matches the marine's job. The five Chapters are all different, and none has a
drawback that affects a Primaris marine, except the Dark Angels' Tradition. Ultramarines were left out on purpose:
their *Pride of Ultramar (Tradition)* sets starting Wrath to 1 (Core p.74), and this brief calls for Wrath 2.

- Space Wolves (Sergeant): free Dual Wield covers his pistol and sword.
- Raven Guard (Disruptor): better Stealth.
- Imperial Fists (Horde-Slayer): rerolls against Kommandos in cover.
- Dark Angels (Marksman): rerolls when he Aims.
- Blood Angels (Blademaster): rerolls in melee.

## Rules shared by all five

**Tier, XP, Rank.**
- Tier 4 characters start with 400 XP (Core p.20).
- All five start at Rank 1 (Core p.36: "Unless the GM says otherwise, you start at Rank 1"; for the Sergeant,
  Core p.148: "You start the game at Rank 1 if you Ascend at character creation").
- So everywhere below, **+Rank = +1** and **+Double Rank = +2**.

**XP cost tables** (Core pp.24–25). These builds used them.

| Rating | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Attribute, total XP | 0 | 4 | 10 | 20 | 35 | 55 | 80 |
| Attribute, step up to this rating | 0 | 4 | 6 | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |

| Rating | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Skill, total XP | 2 | 6 | 12 | 20 | 30 | 42 |
| Skill, step up to this rating | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 | 12 |

**Other creation rules.**
- **Species maximums, Primaris Astartes** (Core p.25): S 12, T 12, A 9, I 9, Wil 10, Int 10, Fel 8, Spd 9.
  Every build is within them.
- **Tree of Learning** (Core p.26): "You must have points in a number of Skills as the highest Rating your character has
  in a single Skill." Each sheet shows this check.
- **Wrath:** "You start every session with 2 Wrath Points" (Core p.164). **All five start each session with Wrath 2.**
  You also earn 1 Wrath for completing your session Objective (Core p.164).

**Species: Primaris Astartes** (Core p.29). [GM] All five use this species (kill-team.md decision 1). Size: Average. Speed: 7.
- **Defender of Humanity:** "Add +Rank Icons to any successful attack against a Mob."
- **Honour the Chapter (Primaris):** "You are subject to the orders of your chapter master, and must honour the beliefs and
  traditions of your chapter. Your Resolve increases by +1. As a Primaris, you ignore any impurities in your Chapter
  Gene-Seed, and also gain +3 Wounds."
- **Space Marine Implants:** "You are immune to the Bleeding Condition. You gain +1 bonus dice to any test related to one of
  the 22 implants (p.75) if the GM agrees it is appropriate."
- Chapter traits with "(Gene-seed)" in the title are ignored for Primaris (Core p.71).

**Background** (Core p.30, table on p.72). Each marine picks one Origin, one Accomplishment and one Goal. Each gives +1 to the
listed trait. Choosing instead of rolling d3 is allowed: the tables are there "to fill in your character's past, or to inspire a
background you devise yourself" (Core p.30).

**Adeptus Astartes Objectives** (Core p.72). At the start of each session, roll d6. Complete the Objective to earn 1 Wrath.

| d6 | Objective |
|---|---|
| 1 | Dedicate a victory in combat to the Primarch (or if unknown, honour) of your Chapter. |
| 2 | Apply the wisdom of the Codex Astartes to a situation. |
| 3 | Clarify your duty — or lack thereof — in the current circumstances. |
| 4 | Speak a motto or saying of your Chapter. |
| 5 | Practice one of the traditions or rituals of your Chapter. |
| 6 | Ruminate on the divide between Astartes and mortals. |

**Languages** (Core p.31): Low Gothic, plus High Gothic free (it needs the ADEPTUS ASTARTES keyword).

**Ammo** (Core p.229).
- "At character creation all of your weapons are fully loaded and you have 3 additional Ammo of any type."
- "As long as you have special Ammo loaded in your weapon you gain all of the benefits listed for that special Ammo."
- [GM] Each marine's 3 spare Ammo includes 1 point of one special Bolt round type (Core pp.230–231).

**Powered armour** (Core p.232, Powered (X)): "you gain a Strength bonus equal to the rating."
- Melee Damage below is (S)+X, where S is the Strength total including the Powered bonus.
- Thrown range is S × 4 m.

**Keywords.** Each sheet lists IMPERIUM, ADEPTUS ASTARTES, PRIMARIS and the Chapter name. [GM] Each marine also has
**DEATHWATCH**, for Influence with the Ordo Xenos (kill-team.md decision 3).

| Trait | Effect |
|---|---|
| Assault | Can fire as part of a Sprint, at +2 DN. |
| Blast (X) | Pick a point and make a DN 3 BS Test. Radius X m; against a Mob, it hits ½X of them. Can't Shift for damage. |
| Brutal | ED results: 1–2 = 0, 3–4 = 1, 5–6 = 2 damage. |
| Heavy (X) | Needs Strength X, or +2 DN. Brace (Core p.188) negates it. |

| Sniper (X) | When you Aim: +1 bonus die and +X ED. |

| Supercharge | Optional. +3 ED on a hit, but a Complication deals 1d6 Mortal Wounds to you. |

- Range bands: +1 die at Short range; beyond Medium range, the target gains +2 Defence (Core p.207).
- **Aim** (Core p.188): give up your Movement for +1 bonus die to ranged attacks.
- **Charge** (Core p.188): move up to double Speed (at least 4 m) and attack with +1 bonus die.

## Book ambiguities and errata found

1. **VoA p.34 Intercessor is misprinted:**
   - It costs 357 XP.
   - It gives Int 5, Tech 5, Leadership, Scholar and Survival.
   - Its Wargear is the Techmarine kit (Artificer armour, servo-arm, Omnissian Axe).
   - Its suggested purchases are the Eliminator's.
   - The correct block is used instead: Core p.117 (300 XP).
2. **VoA pp.32, 33, 37:** the Eliminator, Incursor and Reiver list their species as "Adeptus Astartes", although they have
   the PRIMARIS keyword.
3. **The Bolt Rifle's PRIMARIS keyword:**
   - Core p.217 gives the Bolt Rifle the PRIMARIS keyword; VoA p.67 drops it.
   - This decides whether Intercessor Focus works. Core p.217 is used here.
   - The Heavy Bolter has no PRIMARIS keyword in either book, so Intercessor Focus never applies to a Heavy Intercessor's
     main weapon.
4. **Weapon keywords that differ between the books:**
   - **Power Sword:** Core p.213 gives POWER FIELD, IMPERIUM, **AELDARI**; VoA p.62 gives ADEPTUS ASTARTES. VoA is used.
   - **Astartes Combat Knife:** Core p.211 gives BLADE, ADEPTUS ASTARTES; VoA p.62 gives IMPERIUM, ADEPTUS ASTARTES.
   - **Relic Blade** (VoA p.62) has no POWER FIELD keyword, so Armourbane would not work with it.
   - **Astartes Sniper Rifle:** Core p.219 gives PROJECTILE; VoA p.67 gives **BOLT**. VoA is used, which allows Bolt rounds.
5. **No stats in either book** for: Bolt Sniper Rifle, Occulus Bolt Carbine, Bolt Carbine, Divinator-class Auspex,
   Transpectral Combat Visor, Camo Cloak, Shock Grenades, Smoke Grenades, Haywire Mine. VoA p.75 gives no Value or Rarity
   for the Omni-Scrambler.
6. **Core p.117 Intercessor breaks the Tree of Learning as printed:** it has BS 6 with only 5 trained skills (Core p.26).
   The Horde-Slayer buys a sixth skill (Intimidation 2) to comply.
7. **Rubicon Primaris grants Built Tough, which requires Survival 2+ (VoA p.46),** but the Tactical Space Marine has
   Survival 1. The book doesn't say whether a granted talent ignores requirements. The Sergeant buys Survival 2 to be safe.
8. **VoA p.43 errors:**
   - It calls the Ascension Packages "Talents" ("you may purchase one of the following Talents").
   - Its "Primaris Ascension packages (pg 34)" are actually the Doctrines on p.35.
9. **Kraken Bolt Rounds** (Core p.231) say only "Effect: AP -2". It is unclear whether that replaces the weapon's AP or adds
   to it.
10. **Explosive Fury** (VoA p.33): "+Rank bonus extra damage" could mean flat damage or ED.
11. **Furious Charge** (Core p.134): "+Rank to any melee attack" doesn't say what the +Rank is added to.
12. **Page references differ from where the rules actually are:**
    - Kraken Bolt Rounds are on Core **p.231**, not pp.229–230.
    - The Core Aim rule is on p.188, although Grim Resolve cites p.189.
    - Chapter traits are on Core pp.73–74 (pp.71–74 including the intro). The Space Marine implants list is p.75.
13. **The Ultramarines "Tactical Versatility" Chapter trait** (Shift for Glory twice, Core p.74) has the same name as the
    Tactical Space Marine **archetype ability** (roll the Critical table twice, Core p.113). They are different rules.
